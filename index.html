Our Story

We believe premium shouldn't be exclusive—it should be the standard. LUXE was founded to bridge the gap between exceptional design and everyday life.

Redefining Premium

Founded in 2020, LUXE started with a simple idea: curate the world's finest products and make them accessible to everyone who values quality. We partner directly with artisans, innovators, and designers who share our obsession with detail.

Every product in our collection meets rigorous standards for design, durability, and sustainability. We personally test each item before it ever reaches your doorstep.

Today, we serve over 50,000 customers across 40 countries, and we're just getting started.
